**Re-checked, no change:** Opus 5.5 effort advice (Bug Hunt rises with every step, 22.3 → 41.7 fixed, at 1.4–1.9x cost per step); Sonnet 5.5 weak at high and best at max (Bug Hunt 34.3 high, 51.3 max; VulcanBench 86.8 high, 92.0 max); Astra low or medium is enough (VulcanBench 87.4 at low in 4.2 min); Luna needs max (VulcanBench: 0 of 23 tasks passed at low and medium); DeepSeek V4.1 Flash and MiMo V2.6 Pro as economy workers (FrontierCode 44.0% at about $0.5, chart-read, and 45.4% at $0.18); Grok 4.7 stays unplaced (Bug Hunt 29 fixed for $22.96+ floor, FrontierCode 33.8%), but VulcanBench ranks Grok 4.7 first, which is contested because Grok 4.6 helps judge that benchmark.

What matters for model selection:

- **Haiku 5.5 is a subagent model, not a cheaper Luna.** Its list price equals Luna's, but it spends 3x (AA, max effort) to 4x (AA FrontierCode, 197k vs 48k tokens) the output tokens, so cost per task depends on effort and prompt size S2 S35. On FrontierCode it ties Luna at 41.8% vs 41.9% for roughly 3.7x the cost (Haiku's cost is chart-read and excludes the >100K step-up); on VulcanBench it beats Luna by 11 points at max and ties Sonnet 5.5 at 3.6x lower cost; Sol 6.1 is still 1.9–3.8x cheaper than Haiku there S10.
- **Where Haiku 5.5 wins:** tight-spec, test-checkable subagent jobs (99.8% on graded code at $1.02 vs Sonnet $5.41 and Opus $11.42, n=21 jobs) S5; UI / browser agent loops (1/5 the cost of Sonnet and Opus, 20–27 s slower) S6; extraction (28 of 40 contracts vs Luna 26, at $1.39 per 1,000) S8; speed is mixed: faster than Luna in three tests (Bug Hunt 2–3x, a browser task 4x, an e2e suite) and slower in two (a 3D build 5 min vs 55 s, decisions 2.38 s vs 0.35 s); 2x faster than Sonnet 5.5 in one test and 20 s slower in another S36 S37 S38 S39 S40 S41 S6.
- **Where it loses:** vague briefs (hero-section design 32/50 vs Opus 43) S15; hours-long sessions ("forgets parts of the context") S12; bug hunting at the top of the board (21.5 of 105 for $5.83 vs Sol 6.1 medium 29 for $1.76) S11; classification-style decisions (174/262 vs Luna 195/262, vendor-run) S40; refusals and tool calling in a user-simulation workload S42, with AA saying its AutomationBench score is understated by an over-refusal bug S2.
- **Wire the Claude engine correctly or the saving disappears.** The built-in Explore subagent inherits the main model; name a user subagent exactly `Explore` with `model: haiku` S27 S43. Subagent cache lives 5 minutes; either run one-shot subagents or set `subagentPromptCacheTtl` S25. The advisor re-reads the transcript uncached (54% of one bill) S28. Keep Haiku prompts under 100K tokens or the whole request bills 5x S44.
- **Review quality is a process problem first.** Fresh-context reviewers found 17–18 of 23 issues where the writing session found 3; reviewers that ran the code found the logic bugs readers missed (0 of 8 by reading); Opus 5.5 was the best price-quality reviewer; Sonnet 5.5 varied 2/8 to 4/8 on identical input; two strong reviewers overlapped on 10 of 20 findings, so a second lineage adds coverage S45 S20 S46.
- **Sol 6.1 is the value anchor in every independent coding dataset this week:** FrontierCode 49.1% at $0.81 (Opus 5.5 52.2% at $10.15) S9; VulcanBench 86–89 at $0.31–0.46 S10; Bug Hunt 42.7 fixed for $4.35 at xhigh S11. Complaints are speed per token and over-checking, not quality S47.
- **Multi-agent teams rarely pay:** Vals measured 1.8x–5.1x cost for a significant gain in 1 of 4 team-vs-single comparisons S29; Hexakin found Opus alone above the Opus+Haiku team where tasks were loose S48.
- **Treat the viral official-tip posts as noise.** One template (Official <vendor> tip for <tool>: stop burning <big model> context on tasks <small model> can swarm) appears for Claude and for Codex with invented flags (`--subagents haiku`, `--agent adversary`) from at least 14 accounts with 3 to 115K followers S49 S50 S51.
